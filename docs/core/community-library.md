# Community Library

The Community Library holds movements developed and contributed by Saberists, schools, and clubs outside the founding Core and Extended libraries. Community movements are credited to their contributing school or individual. Over time, a movement here may be promoted into the Extended or Core libraries.

No movements have been submitted to the Community Library yet.

## Contributing a movement

If your school or club has built its own CM and wants to share it with the wider SaberCraft community, see [Contribute](../contributing-and-submissions.md) for how submissions work.

Schools building their own internal CM libraries first — without submitting to the shared standard — can also see [For Schools](../for-schools.md) and the [Independent School Example](../examples/academy-a-example.md).
